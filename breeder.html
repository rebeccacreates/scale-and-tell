    My story 💜

    I started Herps & Hers, and I keep Oro & Kane (ball pythons) and Roux (corn snake).

    The day I lost track. My “system” was notes on the tanks and my notes app, and I never kept up with it. Then one feed night I stood there with a thawed rat and honestly couldn’t remember if Oro had eaten Tuesday or Thursday.

    The trip that scared me. When I had to go out of town, someone else had to feed them for me. I spent the whole trip filled with anxiety. Did they eat for her? Did they feed successfully? Are they warm enough for digestion? Is humidity right?

    The night I felt helpless. One feed night, Oro was in shed and taking a while to finish his meal. When I came back, he had a piece of substrate stuck in his mouth, and I panicked. We’d never had an issue like that with any of them, and I had no one to ask in real life. So there I was posting in groups and vet groups and watching YouTube videos, my anxiety sky high and my poor baby uncomfortable. That night is why Scale & Tell has vet resources, urgent-care warning signs and a care library built right in.

    Then the group spoke up. When I asked the Herps & Hers community I founded for like-minded, empowered female reptile enthusiasts, keepers, breeders and rescues all said the same thing: “I wish there was ONE app that just did all of it.”

    So I built it. Scale & Tell is the app I wished I had: every feeding, shed and weight in a few taps, a sitter sheet I can send in one tap, and alerts when temps drift. And because it’s built with our community, when members ask for something, I build it, often same day.

    I’d love for you to try it free for 7 days.

    — Rebecca 💜
