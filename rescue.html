    How does verification work?

    Start your free trial, then upload your 501(c)(3) letter in Business Tools → Rescue Profile. We review it, usually within a day, and drop your price to half off automatically. No need to sign up again.

    Verified 501(c)(3)s pay $39.99 a year, about $3.33 a month, and your first 7 days are free. Your donate button, public rescue page and year-end impact report are there to help you raise more for your animals. Plus, save 10% on the RodentPro feeders you already buy.

    Our volunteers aren’t techy.

    They don’t have to learn the whole app. A foster with a login opens straight to their own foster animals, and logging a feeding takes 3 taps. Fosters without a login get a care sheet link: nothing to download.

    What happens after 7 days?

    Your plan starts at the price you picked. We email you a reminder 3 days before your trial ends.

    How do I cancel?

    In the app, tap Menu → My Account → Manage plan → Cancel. Cancel before your trial ends and you’re never charged. Or just email support@scaleandtell.app and we’ll do it for you.

    Why do you need my card for a free trial?

    So your plan keeps going without a hiccup if you love it. You’re not charged today, and if you cancel before day 8 you pay nothing. Payments are handled securely by Stripe; we never see your card number.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes: iPhone, Android and computers. Add it to your home screen like an app. App Store and Google Play are coming.
