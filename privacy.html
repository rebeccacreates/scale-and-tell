Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Scale & Tell is a reptile care journal made by Herps & Hers. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time. Questions: support@scaleandtell.app

Who we are

Scale & Tell (scaleandtell.app) is operated by the founder of Scale & Tell and Herps & Hers. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Scale & Tell, and “you” means anyone who uses the app or website.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored encrypted; we can never see them.
  • Your journal: anything you enter, such as animal profiles, feedings, sheds, weights, health and vet notes, photos, enclosure and rack details, pairings and clutches, checklists and reminders.
  • Member code: if you unlock access with a Herps & Hers member code, we record that your account has member access.
  • Scale Hub how-tos: if you submit a Bioactive or DIY how-to, we keep what you wrote and the credit name you choose. Approved how-tos are shown to other keepers.
  • Business Tools (breeder and rescue plans): team members you invite (their email), your public page details and social links, your logo and brand color, your rescue profile including an EIN if you give one for 501(c)(3) verification, and messages people send through your public page (their name, email and message). Your public page, rescue listing and intake status are shown publicly when you choose to list them.
  • Team logins: people you invite can see and edit your shared journal until you remove them.
  • Support messages: anything you send to our support email.

Information collected automatically

  • Reminder notifications: if you turn on phone reminders, your device gives us a push subscription address, plus your time zone and reminder times, so alerts arrive on time.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting and database providers keep standard logs such as IP address, browser type and request times, for security and troubleshooting.
  • On your device: the app keeps a copy of your journal in your browser so it loads quickly and works when your connection drops.
  • Screenshot import: when you import a care log from a screenshot, the text is read on your own device. The image is not uploaded to us unless you save it as a journal photo.

Payments

Lifetime access and the Founding Keeper Pack are paid through PayPal. PayPal handles your card or PayPal account details; we never see or store them. We receive only a confirmation of the payment (order number, amount and status) so we can unlock your account and handle refunds.

Affiliate links

The Reptile Products section and our website include affiliate links, including Amazon and Chewy. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. When you tap one of these links, the store and affiliate network (Amazon Associates, and Impact for Chewy and other partners) may use cookies or similar technology to credit the referral. Our website loads Impact’s tracking script for this purpose. These companies handle that information under their own privacy policies. Any commissions or profit from paid accounts go into upgrading the app, donations to organizations of Herps & Hers’ choice, and/or hosting in-person Herps & Hers events.

How we use information

  • To run your journal, sync it across your devices and keep it backed up.
  • To send the care reminders you turned on.
  • To send account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset) and reply to support requests.
  • To confirm member access and purchases.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, show you ads based on your journal, or share your journal with other keepers. Community spaces (the Herps & Hers Facebook group and the Keeper Lounge) are on Facebook and Messenger, separate from your private journal, and follow Meta’s terms.

Services we use

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database and file storage for your journal.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare: hosting the website, our domain and email forwarding.
  • Resend: sending account emails.
  • PayPal: payments and yearly plans.
  • Govee (only if you connect it): we store the Govee API key you give us on our server and use it only to read your own sensors’ temperature and humidity. Disconnect anytime to delete the key.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: delivering reminder notifications to your device.
  • jsDelivr: delivering open-source code libraries the app uses.
  • Impact and Amazon Associates: affiliate link tracking, as described above.

These providers process information only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and journal while your account is open, so it is there when you come back. When your account is deleted, we delete your journal, photos and reminder settings from our active systems. Copies may remain in short-term backups for a limited time, and we keep payment confirmations as long as needed for tax, accounting and refund purposes.

Your choices

  • Export: use Data & Backup in the app to download a copy of your journal at any time.
  • Correct: edit or remove any entry in the app.
  • Delete your account: use Privacy & Account in the app to prepare a deletion request, or see account deletion, and send it to support@scaleandtell.app from the email on your account. We will confirm once it is done.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app or in your phone’s settings.
  • Access requests: email us to ask what information we hold about you.

Security

Your journal is private to your account, and database rules prevent other users from reading it. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don’t use anywhere else.

Children

Scale & Tell is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, when appropriate, let you know in the app or in the Herps & Hers group.

Contact

Email support@scaleandtell.app with any privacy question or request.
