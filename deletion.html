Delete your Scale & Tell account

Want a copy first? Open the app and use Data & Backup → Export before deleting. Once your account is deleted, your journal can’t be recovered.

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Scale & Tell and tap ☰ Menu → More → Privacy & Account.
  2. Under Delete my account, type DELETE and tap Permanently delete my account.

Your account and everything in it is deleted right away.

Or ask us to do it

Email support@scaleandtell.app from the email address on your account. We’ll confirm by email once it’s done. Never send your password or backup files; we will never ask for them.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Your journal: animals, care history, notes, photos, racks, pairings, clutches, checklists and reminders.
  • Your reminder notification settings.

What we keep

  • Payment confirmations (order number, amount, date), as needed for tax, accounting and refund records.
  • Copies in short-term backups, which expire on their own.

Deleting your account does not refund a past purchase. For refund questions, email us.
